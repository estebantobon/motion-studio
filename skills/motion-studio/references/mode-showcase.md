# Mode: showcase

A brand reel with little or no copy, 6–15 seconds, often posted as a loop. It sells the feel of the brand, not a specific message.

## Approach
- Words only where they are the visual (a single oversized word, the brand name). No sentences.
- Lean harder on rhythm: cut on every bar, land a hit on most beats.
- Rotate visual approaches aggressively (see the list in mode-ad.md) and push colour changes between every shot.
- The thread element carries the whole piece and resolves into the logo at the end.
- Make it loop: the last frame should flow into the first (same background colour, the thread in the same position) so it replays seamlessly.

## Defaults
4:5 or 1:1, 10s, 120 BPM. With music, cut exactly to the track.
