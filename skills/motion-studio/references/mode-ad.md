# Mode: ad

One message, one call to action, 8–20 seconds. The viewer should be able to repeat the takeaway after one watch.

## Structure
Think of the on-screen text as a single sentence split across shots, read in order:
1. **Hook (first shot, under 2s)**: say plainly what this is about. Someone who scrolls away after two seconds should still know.
2. **Problem or input**: what the viewer has or struggles with.
3. **What happens**: the product or idea doing its thing.
4. **Proof**: show the literal result at least once — the actual output, screen, or product — not just a symbol of it.
5. **Call to action + brand**: one shot (two if the ad is 16s+). It still follows the craft rules (fills the frame, has a move), then holds completely still for the last 0.5s so it reads as a final frame and a thumbnail.

Keep 2–5 words on screen in every shot. Picture the literal thing named: a podcast looks like a podcast (mic, waveform, episode card), a spreadsheet looks like a spreadsheet.

## Visual approaches to rotate through (don't repeat one twice in a row)
- Oversized type that runs off the frame edge
- A simple shape that multiplies, orbits, or grows to fill the frame
- A full-frame pattern (dots, grid, tiles) that ripples or reveals a shape
- A 3D object or particle field (only if @remotion/three works)
- A drawn line or ribbon that travels and wraps
- Text on a circle or curve
- The user's real screenshot or clip inside a device or card, with a push-in or mask reveal
- A number or chart that builds — only with numbers the user supplied

## Defaults
4:5, 12s, 120 BPM silent, six shots of two seconds.
